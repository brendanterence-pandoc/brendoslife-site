Marcela’s Day 3 Shoulder Arthroscopy Recovery Guide

Saturday, October 3, 2026 Surgery: October 1, 2026

This guide supports, but does not replace, the specific instructions provided by the surgeon and surgical center.

Today’s goal: Keep pain controlled, protect the shoulder, keep the dressing clean and dry until the 72-hour point, continue the antibiotic, and watch for signs of infection.

The four rules for Day 3

  • Take pain medicine when pain is developing rather than waiting until it becomes severe. Use only the prescribed dose and interval.
  • No lifting with the operative shoulder/hand and no overhead activity. Use the sling for comfort.
  • Keep the dressing clean and dry until the full 72-hour point after surgery.
  • Take cefadroxil twice daily for the full 7-day course unless the surgeon tells you otherwise.

Pain medicine and stomach comfort

Oxycodone prescription: 5 mg, 1–2 tablets every 6 hours as needed for pain. The label allows up to 10 mg at a dosing time, but the goal is the lowest dose that controls the pain.

  • Take oxycodone with food or a small snack. The discharge packet specifically recommends pain medicine with food.
  • Drink fluids regularly unless another clinician has told her to restrict fluids.
  • If the pain is controlled, she does not need to take oxycodone just because a dosing time is available.
  • Do not take another opioid such as Vicodin/hydrocodone at the same time as oxycodone unless Dr. Page specifically instructed that combination.
  • Do not add Tylenol/acetaminophen unless you know exactly what is in any other prescribed pain medicine and the surgeon/pharmacist has confirmed it is appropriate.
  • No alcohol, driving, or machinery while taking narcotic pain medicine.

Cefadroxil and stomach upset

The surgeon ordered cefadroxil twice daily for one week. Taking it with a meal is a practical way to reduce stomach upset. If you develop repeated vomiting, severe diarrhea, hives, facial/lip swelling, or trouble breathing, contact a clinician promptly; trouble breathing or facial/throat swelling is an emergency.

Day 3 medication timetable

Use this schedule only if she has not taken oxycodone within the previous 6 hours. If she already took a dose later than 3:00 AM, move the oxycodone times forward so each dose is at least 6 hours after the prior dose. Do not wake her overnight solely to take oxycodone if she is sleeping comfortably.

Shoulder care today

  • Sling for comfort.
  • No overhead activity.
  • No lifting anything with the operative shoulder or hand until follow-up.
  • Gentle elbow motion is allowed when the sling is removed.
  • Squeezing a small ball with the hand is encouraged.
  • Dr. Page’s sheet says ice for 2 days, then try heat. If using either, protect the skin and use comfort as the guide.

Dressing and shower

Dr. Page’s shoulder-specific instructions say to wait 72 hours before changing to a lighter dressing and showering. Until that time, keep the dressing clean and dry.

After 72 hours, place Band-Aids over the small portal sites and larger clean dressings over larger incisions as needed. Do not bathe, swim, use a hot tub, or submerge the wounds.

Call Dr. Page if you see

  • Pain that is becoming markedly worse instead of gradually improving.
  • Increasing redness, swelling, foul odor/drainage, or temperature over 100°F.
  • Excessive or bright-red bleeding.
  • A cold, discolored, unusually numb/tingling hand or increasing arm/hand pain.
  • Persistent nausea or vomiting that prevents her from keeping medicines or fluids down.

Emergency evaluation

Severe or prolonged shortness of breath, very slow or shallow breathing, inability to wake normally, or facial/throat swelling warrants emergency evaluation.

Tomorrow / after the 72-hour point

  • Change the original dressing to a lighter clean dressing.
  • Band-Aids may cover the small arthroscopy portal sites; use larger dressings for larger incisions.
  • Showering is allowed after 72 hours. If a wound/dressing gets wet, dry it and replace it with a clean dressing.
  • Continue no lifting, no overhead activity, antibiotic twice daily, and pain medicine only as needed.
Source basis: Premier Surgery Center of Sarasota postoperative instructions and Dr. Steven Page’s shoulder arthroscopy instructions dated 10/01/2026. Where practical medication advice goes beyond the packet—for example, taking cefadroxil with a meal to reduce stomach upset—it is identified as general medication guidance.
